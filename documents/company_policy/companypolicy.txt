COMPANY POLICY

1. Working Hours
Employees normally work Monday to Friday,
9 AM to 6 PM, subject to their employment terms.

2. Code of Conduct
Employees must comply with company policies,
protect confidential information, and behave
professionally toward colleagues and customers.

3. Work From Home
Employees must follow their manager's approval
process for remote work.

4. Expenses
Business expenses must be supported by receipts
and submitted according to the finance process.

5. Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company
information to unauthorized persons.
