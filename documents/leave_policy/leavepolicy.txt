EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave
Eligible full-time employees receive 20 annual
leave days per calendar year in this sample policy.

2. Sick Leave
Employees may request sick leave according to
company rules and applicable local requirements.

3. Leave Application
Employees must provide their employee code,
leave type, start date, end date, and reason.

4. Approval
A leave application is a request, not an approval.
The manager or authorized HR approver must approve it.

5. Weekends and Holidays
The number of leave days is calculated according
to the official company leave calendar.

6. Leave Balance
Leave balances must be verified against the
employee's authoritative HR record.
